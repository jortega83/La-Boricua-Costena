Pernil Asado

A bone-in pork shoulder marinated overnight in garlic, sofrito, citrus, and sazón, then slow-roasted until fall-apart tender with crackling crispy skin, the cuerito everyone fights over.

MARINATE overnight, 12 to 24 hrs ROAST 4 to 5 hrs SERVES 8 to 10
Servings
8
8 lb bone-in pork shoulder
Ingredients
    Getting the skin crispy, the skin only crackles well on bone-in, skin-on shoulder. Pat it bone dry before roasting, and don't be afraid to crank the oven up at the very end, right under the broiler if needed, until it sounds hollow when you knock on it.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Make ahead, the marinade can be made a couple days ahead. The pork itself needs at least 12 hours marinating, overnight to 2 days is even better.

    Store, shredded pernil keeps in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 5 days.

    Reheat, covered in the oven at 325°F with a splash of the pan juices, or in the microwave covered so it doesn't dry out.

    Freeze, shredded meat freezes well for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge before reheating.
