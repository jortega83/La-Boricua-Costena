Juji's Chop Cheese

Bodega classic made with burger patties, seared, chopped fine right on the griddle with onions, and smothered in melted American cheese on a toasted hero.

Receta de Juji
ACTIVE 20 min COOK 15 min MAKES 4 sandwiches
Sandwiches
4
1.5 lbs ground beef
Ingredients
    Juji's tip, chop the patties small and mix them into the onions right on the griddle, don't just crumble them separately. That's what gives chop cheese its texture, half seared crust, half juicy crumble, all mixed together.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Make ahead, this is best made fresh and eaten right away, the meat and cheese don't hold well once assembled.

    Store, leftover chopped meat filling keeps in the fridge for up to 3 days, without the roll.

    Reheat, skillet over medium heat until warmed through, then build a fresh sandwich with a freshly toasted roll.

    Freeze, the cooked meat and onion mixture freezes well for up to 2 months, without cheese or the roll.
