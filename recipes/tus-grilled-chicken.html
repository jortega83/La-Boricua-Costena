Thu's Grilled Chicken

A quick brine locks in moisture, then a lemon-garlic-herb marinade builds the flavor. Pound it even, grill it hot, and it comes off the grates juicy every time.

Receta de Thu
BRINE 30 min MARINATE 30 min to 2 hrs GRILL 10 to 12 min
Servings
4
4 chicken breasts
Ingredients
    Thu's tip, the brine is what makes this juicy, don't skip it even though it adds a step. Pounding the chicken to an even thickness matters just as much, it's the difference between a dry thin end and a perfectly cooked thick end.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Make ahead, the chicken can brine and marinate the night before, just don't marinate longer than 24 hours or the acid starts to break down the texture.

    Store, cooked chicken keeps in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days.

    Reheat, gently in a skillet over medium-low with a splash of broth, or microwave covered so it doesn't dry out.

    Freeze, cooked chicken freezes well for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge before reheating.
