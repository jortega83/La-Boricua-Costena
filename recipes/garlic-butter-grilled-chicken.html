Garlic Butter Grilled Chicken

Pounded even, seasoned simple, grilled hot with cross-hatch sear marks, and finished with a garlic parsley butter that melts right in as it rests. Great on its own or piled onto a bun with pepper jack.

ACTIVE 15 min GRILL 7 to 8 min SERVES 4
Servings
4
4 chicken breasts
Ingredients
    Sandwich option, toast the bun, melt a slice of pepper jack over the hot chicken, and pile it on with a swipe of extra garlic butter. Works just as well with boneless chicken thighs, grill in the same way, just watch for doneness since thighs cook a little differently than breasts.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Make ahead, the garlic butter can be made a few days ahead and kept refrigerated, soften before using.

    Store, cooked chicken keeps in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days.

    Reheat, gently in a skillet over medium-low with a small pat of butter, or in the microwave covered so it doesn't dry out.

    Freeze, cooked chicken freezes well for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge before reheating.
