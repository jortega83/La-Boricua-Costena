Johanna's Chocolate Chip Cookies

Big bakery-style cookies with a chilled, portioned dough baked straight from frozen for a crisp edge and a soft, gooey center.

Receta de Johanna
CHILL 1 hr FREEZE until solid MAKES 16 cookies
Cookies
16
2 cups butter, 6 cups flour
Ingredients
    This is a base dough, it's built to be customized. Swap in 2 cups of any mix-ins you like, chocolate chips, candy, chopped cookie pieces, added with the dry ingredients. Any wet flavors like vanilla or purées go in with the eggs. The chill is where the flavor and texture happen, don't skip it.
    Storage & leftovers ⌄

    Make ahead, the whole point of freezing the portioned dough is to have cookies ready whenever you want them, no need to bake the full batch at once.

    Store raw dough, frozen dough balls keep in a freezer bag for up to 3 months, bake straight from frozen anytime.

    Store baked cookies, airtight container at room temperature for up to 5 days.
