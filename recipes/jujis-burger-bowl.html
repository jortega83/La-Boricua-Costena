Juji's Burger Bowl

All the flavor of a classic burger, no bun required. Seasoned beef, crispy roasted sweet potatoes, crunchy veggies, and a creamy tangy sauce made with Greek yogurt instead of mayo.

Receta de Juji
PREP 10 min COOK 20 min SERVES 4
Servings
4
1 lb lean ground beef
Ingredients
    Juji's tip, salt the sweet potatoes after they come out of the oven, not before. Salting first draws out moisture and makes them soggy instead of crispy. No ground beef? Ground turkey, chicken, or bison all work great here too.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Make ahead, great for meal prep. Cook the beef and roast the sweet potatoes ahead, then assemble fresh bowls through the week.

    Store, keep the beef, sweet potatoes, and sauce in separate airtight containers in the fridge for up to 4 days.

    Keep fresh, store the romaine, tomato, onion, and pickles separately so they stay crisp, add them just before eating.

    Reheat, warm the beef and sweet potatoes in the microwave, then build the bowl with the cold, fresh components.
