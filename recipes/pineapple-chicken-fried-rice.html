Pineapple Chicken Fried Rice

Sweet and a little spicy, with ground chicken, scrambled eggs, sweet pineapple, and cilantro. One skillet, ready in about 35 minutes, better than takeout.

PREP 15 min COOK 20 min SERVES 6
Servings
6
4 cups day-old rice
Ingredients
    Good to know, use chilled, day-old rice, not freshly cooked. Fresh rice is too moist and turns soggy and clumpy in the skillet. If you're short on time, spread fresh rice on a sheet pan and chill it in the freezer for 15 minutes before using. No ground chicken on hand? Leave it out and serve this as a side dish instead.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Make ahead, great for meal prep, cook a full batch and portion it out for the week.

    Store, airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days.

    Freeze, freezes well for up to 3 months.

    Reheat, thaw overnight in the fridge if frozen, then warm small portions in the microwave for about a minute, or a full batch in a skillet over medium heat, stirring frequently. If it's dry, stir in a splash of water, broth, or soy sauce.
