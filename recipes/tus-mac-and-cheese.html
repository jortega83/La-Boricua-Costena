Tu's Mac & Cheese

A proper roux-based cheese sauce with sharp cheddar and gruyère, baked under a crisp panko-parmesan crust. Creamy inside, golden on top.

Receta de Tu
ACTIVE 25 min BAKE 20 to 25 min SERVES 8
Servings
8
1 lb elbow macaroni
Ingredients
    Tu's tip, shred your own cheese off the block, pre-shredded bags are coated in anti-caking starch that keeps the sauce from going fully smooth. Warm the milk before adding it to the roux so the sauce comes together without lumps.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Make ahead, assemble up to the point of baking, cover, and refrigerate up to a day ahead. Add the breadcrumb topping right before baking and add a few extra minutes since it'll start cold.

    Store, airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days.

    Reheat, covered in a 325°F oven with a splash of milk stirred in, or microwave individual portions.

    Freeze, freezes well unbaked for up to 2 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge, top with breadcrumbs, then bake.
