Taco Chicken Potato Bowl

Crispy taco-seasoned potatoes, seared chicken, fresh pico de gallo, and a smoky coriander lime sauce. High protein, low fuss, built for the week ahead.

ACTIVE 20 min POTATOES 18 to 24 min MAKES 5 bowls
Bowls
5
1.5 lbs chicken, 2 lbs potatoes
Ingredients
    Good to know, an air fryer gets the potatoes crispiest, but a 425°F oven works well too, just add a few extra minutes. Diced small and even is key for quick, uniform cooking.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Store, keep the potatoes and chicken together in airtight containers in the fridge for up to 4 days.

    Keep separate, store the pico de gallo and sauce separately, add them fresh when serving.

    Reheat, microwave the potatoes and chicken together in 1 minute bursts until hot, then top with the fresh, cold components.

    Freeze, the cooked chicken and potatoes freeze well for up to 2 months.
