Camarones al Maní

Shrimp simmered in a creamy peanut sauce built on an achiote sofrito, with crispy patacones folded right into the stew at the end. A Pacific coast specialty, rich, smoky, and a little sweet from the peanut.

ACTIVE 30 min SIMMER 20 min SERVES 4
Servings
4
1.1 lbs shrimp, shell-on
Ingredients
    Good to know, chillangua (also called culantro or recao) is a Pacific coast herb, if you can't find it, cilantro is a fine substitute, use a bit more since it's less intense. Natural, unsweetened peanut butter works well in place of peanut paste. No frozen patacones on hand? Use our Patacones Clásicos recipe and fry a fresh batch instead.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Make ahead, the shrimp broth and peanut sauce base can be made a day ahead and refrigerated, add the shrimp and patacones fresh when reheating.

    Store, airtight container in the fridge for up to 2 days, the patacones will soften over time.

    Reheat, gently on the stovetop over low heat, adding a splash of water or broth if the sauce has thickened too much.
