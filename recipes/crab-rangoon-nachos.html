Crab Rangoon Nachos

All the flavor of crab rangoon, piled onto crispy fried wonton chips instead of stuffed and folded. Warm, creamy crab and cream cheese filling, finished with sweet chili sauce and spicy mayo.

ACTIVE 25 min MAKES 15 nachos SERVES 4 to 6
Wonton sheets
30
Makes about 15 nachos
Ingredients
    Good to know, gochugaru gives a milder, fruitier heat than regular chili powder, use whichever you have. Lump crab meat gives the best texture, but imitation crab works in a pinch for a budget version.
    Storage & leftovers ⌄

    Best fresh, assemble and serve right away, the chips soften once topped with the warm filling.

    Make ahead, the filling can be made a day ahead and refrigerated, warm it up before serving. Fry the wonton chips fresh.

    Store leftovers, keep the filling separately in the fridge for up to 3 days, don't store assembled nachos.
