Ensalada Rusa Clásica

Potatoes, carrots, and peas in a bright mayonnaise dressing, with apple and celery for crunch and sweetness. The classic version found across Latin America, perfect alongside grilled meats.

ACTIVE 45 min CHILL 1 hr SERVES 8
Servings
8
3.5 lbs potatoes
Ingredients
    Good to know, some cooks skip the apple, but the sweet crunch is what makes this version special. Other traditional add-ins include green beans, corn, diced beets, hard-boiled egg, or shredded chicken, add whatever sounds good.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Make ahead, perfect for picnics and parties, can be made a day ahead.

    Store, airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days.

    Keep it fresh, add the apple close to serving time if making far ahead, so it doesn't brown.
