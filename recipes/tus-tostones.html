Tu's Tostones

Green plantains fried twice, smashed flat in between, for a crispy outside and tender middle. Dip them in garlic water before the second fry for extra flavor and crunch.

Receta de Tu
ACTIVE 20 min FRY 10 min SERVES 4
Servings
4
4 green plantains
Ingredients
    Tu's tip, use plantains that are truly green, not yellow, or they won't hold their shape when smashed. Serve hot with mojo, ajilimójili, or just a squeeze of lime and extra salt.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Make ahead, tostones don't hold well once fried, best made fresh right before serving.

    First fry ahead, you can do the first fry and smash a few hours ahead, then finish with the second fry just before eating.

    Store, leftovers keep in the fridge for a day or two, but they lose their crunch.

    Reheat, oven or air fryer at 375°F for a few minutes to re-crisp, avoid the microwave.
