Creamy Mashed Potatoes

Yukon Golds riced or mashed smooth, folded with warm butter and cream. Simple, no shortcuts, exactly the way a good side dish should be.

ACTIVE 10 min COOK 15 to 20 min SERVES 6
Servings
6
3 lbs Yukon Gold potatoes
Ingredients
    Optional add-ins, stir in one of these once everything's mashed and seasoned: blue cheese and sautéed red onion, crispy bacon and diced green chilies, or minced chipotle peppers with shredded Monterey Jack.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Make ahead, can be made a day or two in advance. Let cool, refrigerate, then bring back to room temp for a couple hours before reheating.

    Store, airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days.

    Reheat, stovetop over low heat with a splash of milk or cream, or covered in a 350°F oven until warmed through.

    Freeze, mashed potatoes can lose some texture when frozen, best enjoyed fresh or refrigerated.
