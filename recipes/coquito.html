Coquito

Puerto Rico's Christmas eggnog, no eggs, built on a warm spiced tea of cinnamon, star anise, and cloves blended with coconut and rum. Blend it, chill it, and let it round out overnight. Serve cold with a cinnamon stick.

Receta de Johanna
ACTIVE 25 min CHILL 4+ hrs, best overnight ABV adjustable
Servings (4 oz each)
12
≈ 6 cups
Ingredients
    Johanna's tip, the spiced tea is what sets this version apart, don't skip letting it cool before blending or it can curdle the milks. Taste before bottling and adjust the rum to your crowd, it always tastes a little stronger once it's had a few hours to chill and settle.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Make ahead, this is meant to be made ahead. It tastes best after resting at least a few hours, ideally overnight.

    Store, keep refrigerated in sealed bottles for up to 2 weeks.

    Shake before serving, it separates a little in the fridge, that's normal.

    Freeze, not recommended, the dairy can separate and change texture once thawed.
