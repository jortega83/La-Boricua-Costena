Chris E's Sourdough

A high-hydration, no-knead loaf built over two days, levain the night before, a long slow bulk ferment, and a hot dutch oven bake for a dark, crackling crust.

Receta de Chris E
ACTIVE ~1 hr, spread over 2 days FERMENT 8 to 10 hrs total MAKES 2 loaves
Loaves
2
1kg strong white + 300g whole grain flour
Ingredients
    Keep your starter happy, feed it once a day with equal parts flour and water if you bake weekly, or give it one big feed and refrigerate it if you bake less often, just take it out and feed it daily for three days before you build your levain. A lively starter should smell like banana yogurt, thick and bubbly, not thin and vinegary.
    No dutch oven? Sprinkle a preheated baking tray with cornmeal, bake the loaf at 250°C (480°F) with a second tray of water underneath to create steam. Remove the water tray after 30 minutes to let the crust brown directly.
    Storage & keeping ⌄

    Rest before slicing, wait at least an hour after baking, the crumb is still setting.

    Keep it sliceable, wrap the loaf tightly in plastic wrap the day after baking.

    Freeze, freeze in slices rather than as a whole loaf, so you can pull out just what you need.

    Extra starter? Use it for crumpets, waffles, or pizza dough instead of discarding it.
