Moros y Cristianos

Cuban black beans and long-grain rice cooked together with crispy bacon, sofrito, and warm spices, so the rice takes on all the flavor of the beans. A classic side, also called Arroz Congri.

PREP 10 min COOK 50 min SERVES 4
Servings
4
15 oz canned black beans
Ingredients
    Good to know, this is the quick version using canned beans. For a darker, more traditional moros, use 6oz dried black beans instead: soak overnight in 3 cups water, then drain, add 5 cups fresh water, and simmer 60 minutes until soft before using 1½ cups of that cooking liquid in place of the canned bean liquid and 1 cup water. Always rinse your rice first.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Make ahead, can be made up to 2 days in advance, just cool completely before refrigerating.

    Store, airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days.

    Reheat, microwave, or stovetop over low heat with a splash of water.

    Serve it, alongside Cuban roast pork (lechón asado), yuca con mojo, or sweet plantains.
