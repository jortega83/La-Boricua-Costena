Fried Red Snapper Costeño

A whole red snapper, scored, marinated in garlic and lime, and fried until deeply golden and crisp, just like the fish stands in Cartagena. Best served with arroz con coco and patacones.

Cartagena style
MARINATE 30 min, up to 2 hrs FRY 10 to 12 min SERVES 2 to 3 per fish
Whole fish
1
1.5 to 2 lb snapper, serves 2 to 3
Ingredients
    Serve it right, plate the hot pargo alongside dark arroz con coco and freshly fried patacones. A simple sliced tomato and avocado salad with lime rounds it out. Ask if you'd like the arroz con coco or patacones recipes added too.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Make ahead, the fish can be scored and marinated up to a day in advance, refrigerated, then fried right before serving.

    Best fresh, fried fish is at its best right out of the oil. It doesn't hold or reheat as well as most dishes.

    Reheat, if needed, use an oven at 375°F for about 10 minutes to help re-crisp it. Avoid the microwave, it turns the skin soft.

    Freeze, don't freeze it fried. If you want to prep ahead, freeze the raw, cleaned fish and thaw fully before scoring and frying.
