Meal Prep #8

Korean Beef Bowl

Ground beef in a quick gochujang soy-ginger-garlic sauce over rice, with sesame cucumbers. Under 20 minutes and freezes beautifully.

ACTIVE 15 min COOK 10 min MAKES 4 bowls
Bowls
4
1 lb ground beef
Ingredients
    Good to know, gochujang varies a lot in spice level by brand, start with less and add more to taste. A fried egg on top is a great, not-required addition.
    Rice ratio, this recipe always uses about 1¾ cups of water for every 1 cup of rice, plus ¼ teaspoon salt per cup of rice. That ratio is already built into the ingredient list above, whatever serving size you pick, the water and salt scale with it automatically.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Store, airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days.

    Reheat, microwave until hot, or a skillet over medium heat.

    Freeze, the cooked beef and sauce freeze well for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge before reheating.
