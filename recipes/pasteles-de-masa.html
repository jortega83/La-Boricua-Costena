Navidad #1

Pasteles Boricua

Puerto Rico's Christmas centerpiece, a savory root vegetable masa filled with stewed meat, wrapped in banana leaf, and boiled until tender. A labor of love, best made with extra hands in the kitchen.

PREP 3 hrs COOK 1 hr MAKES 12 pasteles
Pasteles
12
2½ lbs meat, 4½ lbs root vegetables
Ingredients
    Good to know, yautía, calabaza, and frozen banana leaves are usually found at a Latino or Caribbean market. Grated frozen masa is sometimes available there too, which saves a lot of prep time. Chickpeas can replace the meat for a vegetarian version.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Make ahead, the filling and masa can both be made a day ahead and refrigerated separately before assembling.

    Freeze uncooked, wrapped pasteles freeze great, up to 6 months in a freezer bag. This is the traditional way to stock up for the season.

    Cook from frozen, boil for 75 to 90 minutes instead of 45 to 60.

    Store cooked, refrigerate cooked, unwrapped pasteles in an airtight container for up to 4 days.
