Game Day #2

Loaded Party Nachos

Seasoned beef, refried beans, and melted cheese built in two layers so every single chip gets loaded, not just the ones on top. Finished with fresh pico de gallo, guacamole, and all the fixings.

ACTIVE 25 min BAKE 10 min SERVES 6 to 8
Servings
8
1.5 lbs ground beef
Ingredients
    The real trick, building this in two layers instead of one big pile is what keeps every chip loaded instead of just the top ones. It's a couple extra minutes of work for a much better result. Add cold toppings only after baking, they don't belong in the oven.
    Storage & leftovers ⌄

    Best fresh, nachos don't hold well, assemble and bake right before serving.

    Make ahead, the beef, beans, and pico de gallo can all be made a day ahead and refrigerated separately, then assemble and bake fresh.

    Leftovers, store the beef and bean mixture in the fridge for up to 3 days, don't store assembled nachos.
