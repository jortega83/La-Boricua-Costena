Arroz de Coco
Costeño

Coastal coconut rice built on titoté, coconut milk reduced slow and dark until the oil breaks free. Rich, a little sweet, a little clingy. As it should be.

Receta de Graciana Romero
TIME ~55 min ACTIVE 20 min GRAIN medium or long
Servings
8
4 cups rice
Ingredients
    Titoté tip, caramelize the sugar with a splash of coconut milk first, before adding the rest. It deepens the color and flavor. Some stickiness is normal, especially with medium grain, you want tender, not soupy.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Make ahead, this holds up well made a day in advance. Flavor deepens overnight.

    Store, airtight container in the fridge for up to 5 days.

    Reheat, stovetop or microwave with a splash of water or coconut milk to loosen it back up.

    Freeze, freezes well for up to 2 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge before reheating.
