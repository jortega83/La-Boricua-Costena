Meal Prep #9

Cajun Chicken and Sausage Rice

One-pot jambalaya-style rice with andouille sausage, chicken, and bell peppers, all cooked together in cajun-seasoned rice. Great for batch cooking since it's basically one pan.

ACTIVE 20 min COOK 25 min MAKES 6 bowls
Bowls
6
1 lb chicken, 12 oz sausage
Ingredients
    Good to know, andouille is the traditional sausage here, but any smoked sausage works. This one cooks the rice right in the pot with everything else, so no separate rice pot needed.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Store, airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days.

    Reheat, microwave with a splash of water or broth to loosen it back up, or a covered skillet over medium-low.

    Freeze, freezes well for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge before reheating.
