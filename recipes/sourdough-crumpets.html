Chris E's Sourdough Crumpets

A delicious way to use up excess starter discard. Thick, bubbly batter cooked low and slow in buttered rings, toasty and full of holes for soaking up butter.

Receta de Chris E
ACTIVE 10 min REST 25 min MAKES 8 crumpets
Crumpets
8
1¼ cups starter discard
Ingredients
    Good to know, old, acidic starter discard works great here, no need to feed it first. If your starter isn't very sour yet, a squeeze of lemon juice helps get that classic tang. Buttering the rings well is not optional, skip it and the crumpets will stick.
    Storage & keeping ⌄

    Make ahead, crumpets are great made in advance and toasted later.

    Store, airtight container at room temperature for a day or two, or the fridge for up to a week.

    Freeze, freeze in a single layer, then bag once solid. Toast straight from frozen.
