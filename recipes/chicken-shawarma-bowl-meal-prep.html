Meal Prep #13

Chicken Shawarma Bowl

Spiced chicken thighs seared until charred, over a one-pan turmeric basmati rice, with a fresh sumac salad and a creamy garlic yogurt sauce. Skip the takeout.

MARINATE 30 min, longer is better ACTIVE 40 min SERVES 4
Bowls
4
1.5 lbs chicken thighs
Ingredients
    Good to know, the chicken marinates in the same pan you'll cook everything in, so there's minimal cleanup. Marinate as long as you can, even 30 minutes helps, but a few hours or overnight is better. Chicken breasts work in place of thighs, just watch the cook time since they dry out faster.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Make ahead, great for meal prep, marinate the chicken the night before and the whole thing comes together fast.

    Store, keep the chicken, rice, salad, and sauce in separate containers in the fridge for up to 4 days.

    Reheat, warm the chicken and rice in the microwave, then add the cold, fresh salad and sauce.
