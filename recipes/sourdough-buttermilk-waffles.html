Chris E's Sourdough Buttermilk Waffles

Light, airy, crispy on the outside. An overnight starter-and-buttermilk soak makes these the best waffles you'll make. Eat with bacon, creme fraiche, bananas, and maple syrup.

Receta de Chris E
SOAK overnight ACTIVE 15 min MAKES 8 waffles
Waffles
8
1 cup starter
Ingredients
    Good to know, the starter for the overnight soak doesn't need to be lively or freshly fed, unfed or discard works just fine here. That's what makes this such a good way to use up extra starter.
    Storage & keeping ⌄

    Make ahead, the overnight soak is already built into this recipe, mix it the night before and you're most of the way there by morning.

    Store, cooked waffles keep in the fridge for up to 3 days.

    Freeze, freeze in a single layer, then bag once solid, up to 2 months.

    Reheat, straight from the fridge or freezer in a toaster or oven to bring back the crisp, avoid the microwave.
