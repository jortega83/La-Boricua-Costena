Titi Norks Jalapeño Poppers

Just three ingredients, halved jalapeños stuffed with cream cheese, wrapped in bacon, and baked until crisp. Simple, always a hit, and freezer-friendly.

Receta de Titi Nork
ACTIVE 20 min BAKE 15 to 20 min MAKES 20 poppers
Jalapeños
10
Makes 20 popper halves
Ingredients
    Good to know, wear gloves when working with the peppers. Round-tipped jalapeños tend to run milder, pointed ones tend to be hotter, so pick your peppers accordingly. For crispier bacon, partially cook it first before wrapping, or bake at a slightly lower temperature for longer.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Make ahead, assemble the poppers up to a day ahead, cover, and refrigerate until ready to bake.

    Store, baked poppers keep in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days.

    Freeze, freezes well, either before or after baking. For raw poppers, freeze on a tray until solid, then transfer to a freezer bag.

    Reheat or bake from frozen, bake straight from frozen at 425°F, just add extra time until the bacon is done and everything is heated through.
