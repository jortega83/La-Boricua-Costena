Shrimp Cocktail

Shrimp and langostinos cooked in seasoned broth, tossed in a fresh dressing of tomato sauce, soy sauce, lime juice, and garlic water. A classic beach snack from the coast.

Recipe by Rubí Cárdenas
TIME ~30 min ACTIVE 20 min SERVE COLD
Servings
6
500g shrimp, 250g langostinos
Ingredients
    About the garlic water, it's made by letting crushed garlic cloves sit in water overnight. If you're short on time, use very finely minced garlic instead. That garlic water touch is what gives it that characteristic coastal flavor.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Make ahead, prepare the garlic water the night before. The shrimp can also be cooked and refrigerated a day ahead.

    Store, refrigerated in an airtight container for up to 2 days. Best eaten the same day the dressing is made.

    Doesn't freeze well, the shrimp and vegetables lose their texture once thawed, so avoid freezing it once assembled.
