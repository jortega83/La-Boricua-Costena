Sweet and Sour Chicken

Crispy pan-fried chicken with bell peppers and pineapple in a tangy homemade sauce, better than takeout and ready to portion out for the week in under an hour.

ACTIVE 45 min TOTAL under 1 hr MAKES 4 bowls
Bowls
4
1.5 lbs chicken breast
Ingredients
    Good to know, cornstarch is what gives the chicken its light, crispy coating, don't skip coating it twice (cornstarch, then egg). Keep the sauce and rice stored separately from the chicken until ready to eat so nothing gets soggy.
    Rice ratio, this recipe always uses about 1¾ cups of water for every 1 cup of rice, plus ¼ teaspoon salt per cup of rice. That ratio is already built into the ingredient list above, whatever serving size you pick, the water and salt scale with it automatically.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Store, keep in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days.

    Keep crispy, for best texture, store the sauce separately and toss just before eating if you can.

    Reheat, microwave until hot, or a skillet over medium heat to help bring back some crispness.

    Freeze, the chicken and sauce together freeze well for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge before reheating.
