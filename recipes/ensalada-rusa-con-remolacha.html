Ensalada Rusa con Remolacha

Potato, carrot, and beet in a simple mayonnaise dressing with fresh cilantro. A traditional side dish on the Colombian Caribbean coast, including Cartagena, served ice cold alongside meat and fish.

ACTIVE 30 min CHILL 1 hr SERVES 6
Servings
6
3 medium potatoes
Ingredients
    Good to know, cook the beet separately from the potato and carrot, its strong color and tougher texture need their own pot and timing. Add the beet to the bowl last and toss gently, or it will tint everything pink.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Make ahead, tastes even better after chilling a few hours, good to make the day before.

    Store, airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days.

    Serve it, ice cold, alongside meats, fish, or classic Cartagena dishes.
