Arroz con Gandules

Puerto Rico's national dish, rice and pigeon peas built on sofrito, sazón, and alcaparrado, finished with that coveted crispy pegao on the bottom.

Adapted from Kitchen Gidget
TIME ~45 min ACTIVE 15 min GRAIN medium or long
Servings
8
3 cups rice
Ingredients
    Good to know, alcaparrado is a jarred mix of olives, capers, and pimentos found in the Latin aisle; if you can't find it, use sliced pimiento-stuffed manzanilla olives instead. Ham flavoring/bouillon is optional, leave it out for a vegetarian version.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Make ahead, great for meal prep. Cook a full batch and portion it out for the week.

    Store, airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days.

    Reheat, stovetop or microwave with a splash of water to bring back the moisture.

    Freeze, freezes well for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge, then reheat with a little extra water.
