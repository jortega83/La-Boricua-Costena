Meal Prep #5

Teriyaki Chicken Bowl

Chicken thighs seared and simmered in a sweet-savory homemade teriyaki glaze, over rice with steamed broccoli. One of the most repeated meal prep recipes online, holds up beautifully for days.

ACTIVE 20 min COOK 15 min MAKES 4 bowls
Bowls
4
1.5 lbs chicken thighs
Ingredients
    Good to know, salmon works great in place of chicken here, sear it skin-side down first and reduce the cook time so it doesn't overcook. Toasted sesame oil goes a long way, a little at the end is plenty.
    Rice ratio, this recipe always uses about 1¾ cups of water for every 1 cup of rice, plus ¼ teaspoon salt per cup of rice. That ratio is already built into the ingredient list above, whatever serving size you pick, the water and salt scale with it automatically.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Store, keep the chicken, rice, and broccoli together in airtight containers in the fridge for up to 4 days.

    Reheat, microwave until hot, or a skillet over medium heat with a splash of water to loosen the sauce.

    Freeze, the cooked chicken and sauce freeze well for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge before reheating.
