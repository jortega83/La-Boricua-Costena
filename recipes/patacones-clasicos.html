Patacones Clásicos

Crunchy chips made from twice-fried green plantain, a staple of the Colombian Caribbean coast (yes, the ones from Encanto). Perfect for scooping guacamole, salsa, or hogao.

PREP 10 min COOK 20 min MAKES 10 patacones
Green plantains
2
Makes about 10 patacones
Ingredients
    Good to know, thinner patacones get a nice corn-chip-like crunch, great for dipping. Thicker ones (don't press as hard) hold up better if you're topping them with shredded meat, chicken, or shrimp. In Puerto Rico and the Dominican Republic, these same plantains are called tostones.
    Serve them right, guacamole and hogao (a cooked Colombian tomato sauce) are the classic pairing, spread the hogao on first, then top with guacamole. On the coast, patacones traditionally go alongside fried fish and coconut rice.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Make ahead, patacones don't hold well once fried, best made fresh right before serving.

    First fry ahead, you can do the first fry and flatten a few hours ahead, then finish with the second fry just before eating.

    Healthier option, an air fryer works well if you'd rather skip the deep fry.
