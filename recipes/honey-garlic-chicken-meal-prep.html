Honey Garlic Chicken

Chicken thighs in a sticky honey-garlic-soy glaze with roasted green beans and rice. Simple pantry ingredients, big flavor, kid-friendly.

ACTIVE 15 min COOK 20 min MAKES 4 bowls
Bowls
4
1.5 lbs chicken thighs
Ingredients
    Good to know, the sauce thickens fast once it starts to reduce, keep an eye on it near the end so it doesn't burn. Chicken breast works too, just watch the cook time since it dries out faster than thighs.
    Rice ratio, this recipe always uses about 1¾ cups of water for every 1 cup of rice, plus ¼ teaspoon salt per cup of rice. That ratio is already built into the ingredient list above, whatever serving size you pick, the water and salt scale with it automatically.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Store, airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days.

    Reheat, microwave until hot, or a skillet over medium heat with a splash of water to loosen the glaze.

    Freeze, the cooked chicken and sauce freeze well for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge before reheating.
