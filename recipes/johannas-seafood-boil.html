Johanna's Seafood Boil

King crab, snow crab, and shrimp, boiled with a full crab boil seasoning blend, shocked in ice water to lock in the texture, then finished hot in the pan with a rich garlic butter sauce, the way a real boil spot does it.

Receta de Johanna
ACTIVE 30 min BOIL 15 min SERVES 4
Servings
4
1.5 lbs king crab, 1.5 lbs snow crab, 1 lb shrimp
Ingredients
    What makes this restaurant quality, three things: a real crab boil seasoning packet in the water (not just Old Bay alone), an ice bath right after boiling so the seafood doesn't keep cooking and turn rubbery, and finishing in a hot pan with the sauce instead of just pouring it on cold, that last step is what actually melts the flavor into the meat instead of sitting on the surface.
    Good to know, king and snow crab legs are almost always sold pre-cooked and frozen, so the boil is really just about warming them through and infusing flavor, not cooking from raw. Want it spicier? Add more cayenne to the sauce, or drop it entirely for non-spicy.
    Storage & leftovers ⌄

    Best fresh, this is meant to be eaten hot right out of the pan, the butter sauce firms up as it cools.

    Store, leftovers keep in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 2 days.

    Reheat, gently in a covered pan over low heat, or microwave in short bursts, high heat toughens the seafood.
