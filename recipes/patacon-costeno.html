Patacón Costeño con Todo

The king of Caribbean coast street food, twice-fried green plantain smashed flat and stacked into a giant sandwich with shredded beef, chicken, cheese, and all the toppings. Eaten any hour of the day in Cartagena.

BEEF ~2 hrs ACTIVE 40 min MAKES 4 patacones
Patacones
4
4 green plantains
Ingredients
    Good to know, soaking the smashed plantains briefly in garlic water before the second fry is a coastal trick that adds flavor and extra crunch. Queso costeño is traditional, but mozzarella or a mild white cheese works well too.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Make ahead, the shredded beef and chicken can be cooked a day or two ahead and refrigerated, this is the part that takes the longest.

    Store, shredded meats keep in the fridge for up to 4 days, or freeze for up to 3 months.

    Fry fresh, patacones don't hold well once fried, best assembled and eaten right away.

    Reheat meats, stovetop or microwave with a splash of water, then fry fresh plantains to assemble.
