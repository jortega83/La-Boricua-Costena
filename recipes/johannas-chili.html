Johanna's Famous Chili

Ground beef and pork simmered low and slow with three kinds of beans, tomato, and sweet peppers. A cold weather favorite, topped with cheddar and sour cream.

Receta de Johanna
ACTIVE 25 min SIMMER 45 to 60 min SERVES 8 to 10
Servings
8
2 lbs meat, 3 cans beans
Ingredients
    Bean options, pinto, black, red, pink, kidney, and white all work well, mix and match. Three cans is usually perfect, but more can be added if you like a heartier chili.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Make ahead, chili always tastes better the next day, once the flavors have had time to meld. Great for cooking a day before you need it.

    Store, airtight container in the fridge for up to 5 days.

    Reheat, stovetop over low heat or microwave, adding a splash of water if it's thickened up too much.

    Freeze, freezes well for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge before reheating.
