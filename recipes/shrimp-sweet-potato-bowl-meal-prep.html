Shrimp & Sweet Potato Bowl

Fast, high-protein, and loaded with lean seafood and complex carbs. Cajun-seared shrimp, roasted sweet potato, fresh toppings, and a drizzle of hot honey.

ACTIVE 15 min ROAST 25 min SERVES 4
Bowls
4
1.75 lbs shrimp
Ingredients
    Good to know, a quinoa or rice blend makes this heartier, shredded cabbage or arugula keeps it lighter and lower carb. Swap the pico de gallo for diced pineapple salsa for a sweeter version.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Make ahead, roast the sweet potato and prep the base ahead, sear the shrimp fresh each time for the best texture.

    Store, keep components in separate containers in the fridge for up to 4 days.

    Keep fresh, add the avocado and hot honey right before eating.

    Reheat, the shrimp and sweet potato in the microwave, then build the bowl with the cold, fresh toppings.
