Meal Prep #2

Chipotle Steak Bowl

Smoky chipotle-marinated steak, seared hot and sliced against the grain, over cilantro lime rice with black beans, fajita veggies, and corn salsa. Portions out into containers for the whole week.

MARINATE 2 hrs, up to overnight ACTIVE 40 min MAKES 4 bowls
Bowls
4
1.5 lbs skirt or flank steak
Ingredients
    Good to know, skirt or flank steak works best here, they're thin cuts that take on the marinade well and stay tender when sliced against the grain. Canned chipotle peppers in adobo are the key ingredient, found in the international aisle.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Store, keep the warm components (steak, rice, beans, corn) together in an airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days.

    Keep separate, store fresh toppings like lettuce and any avocado separately so they don't get soggy or brown.

    Reheat, microwave the warm components until heated through, then add cold toppings fresh.

    Freeze, the cooked steak freezes well on its own for up to 3 months.
