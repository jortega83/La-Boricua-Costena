Thu's White Rice

Fluffy medium-grain rice, toasted first in olive oil for a bit of nutty flavor, then steamed until tender. The everyday base for habichuelas guisadas and just about everything else.

Receta de Thu
ACTIVE 5 min COOK 25 min REST 10 min
Servings
6
2 cups rice
Ingredients
    Good to know, medium-grain rice (Arroz Rico or Calrose) is traditional, though long-grain works fine too. If you're using a caldero, a golden crust of pegao will form on the bottom, don't fight it, that's the good part.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Make ahead, cooks in under 30 minutes, but leftovers reheat well if you want to prep ahead.

    Store, airtight container in the fridge for 5 to 7 days.

    Reheat, add a splash of water and microwave, or warm in a covered pan on the stove.

    Freeze, freezes well for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge, then reheat with a little water.

    Leftover idea, day-old arroz blanco is perfect for arroz mamposteao, sautéed with beans, vegetables, and a protein like ham or chorizo.
