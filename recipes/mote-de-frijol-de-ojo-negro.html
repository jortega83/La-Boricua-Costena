Mote de Frijol de Ojo Negro

Black-eyed peas (cabecita negra) simmered with ripe plantain until it partially breaks down, thickening the pot into a creamy, comforting stew seasoned with a simple sofrito. A staple of the Colombian Caribbean coast.

ACTIVE 20 min SIMMER ~1 hr SERVES 6
Servings
6
2 cups black-eyed peas
Ingredients
    Good to know, black-eyed peas cook fairly quickly and don't strictly need soaking, but an hour or two in water beforehand shortens the cooking time if you're in a hurry. Want to make it heartier? Smoked ham hock, salted pork, or cubed ñame (yam) are traditional additions, just simmer them along with the beans from the start.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Make ahead, tastes even better the next day once the flavors settle.

    Store, airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days.

    Reheat, stovetop over low heat or microwave, with a splash of water if it's thickened up too much.

    Freeze, freezes well for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge before reheating.
