Spicy Vodka Chicken Parm

Crispy fried chicken cutlets topped with a fiery, creamy vodka tomato sauce and a heavy layer of melted mozzarella and provolone. Chicken parm's bolder cousin.

ACTIVE 35 min MAKES 4 cutlets SERVES 4
Servings
4
2 large chicken breasts
Ingredients
    Good to know, letting the vodka actually reduce for a minute or two cooks off the harsh alcohol taste and leaves behind depth, don't rush that step. No broiler-safe pan? Melt the cheese over the sauce in a small oven-safe dish instead, then spoon it over the chicken.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Make ahead, the vodka sauce can be made a day ahead and reheated gently. Fry the chicken fresh for the best crunch.

    Store, airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days.

    Reheat, oven at 350°F until warmed through, this keeps the coating crisper than the microwave.
