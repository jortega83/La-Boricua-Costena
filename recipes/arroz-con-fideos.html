Arroz con Fideos

Rice toasted with golden angel hair pasta, simple and comforting. One of the most common side dishes in a Colombian kitchen, the kind of rice everyone grew up on.

ACTIVE 10 min COOK 20 min SERVES 4
Servings
4
1 cup rice
Ingredients
    Good to know, the garlic clove is optional, some families always add it, others keep it plain for picky kids. Water works fine, but chicken or vegetable broth gives it a richer flavor. Break the angel hair into small pieces before toasting so it cooks evenly with the rice.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Make ahead, this comes together quickly, but leftovers reheat well if you want to prep it in advance.

    Store, airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days.

    Reheat, stovetop or microwave with a small splash of water to loosen it back up.

    Freeze, freezes well for up to 2 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge before reheating.
