Pastel de Arroz Cartagenero

Also called tamal costeño, achiote rice layered with stewed pork and chicken, sliced vegetables, and olives, wrapped in vijao leaves and boiled. The star dish of end-of-year celebrations in Cartagena.

ACTIVE 1.5 hrs BOIL 1 to 2 hrs MAKES 8 pasteles
Pasteles
8
1.5 lbs pork, 1.5 lbs chicken
Ingredients
    Good to know, vijao leaves are traditional, but banana leaves work as a substitute if you can't find them. This is a project best shared with extra hands, the guiso, rice, and assembly all go faster with help.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Make ahead, the guiso can be made a day ahead and refrigerated. The rice can also be par-cooked ahead of time.

    Freeze uncooked, wrapped pasteles freeze well for up to 3 months, this is the traditional way to stock up for the holidays.

    Cook from frozen, boil for closer to 2 hours instead of 1.

    Store cooked, refrigerate cooked, unwrapped pasteles in an airtight container for up to 4 days.
