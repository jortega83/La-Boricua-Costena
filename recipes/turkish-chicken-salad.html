Turkish Chicken Salad

Shredded chicken folded into a creamy yogurt and dill dressing, served over crisp iceberg lettuce and finished with walnuts and a drizzle of olive oil. A dish the family made in Cartagena, tracing back to the city's Turkish community.

Closest match, courtesy of Raindrop Foundation Turkish Cooking Class
ACTIVE 20 min COOK 18 min SERVES 5
Servings
5
1 lb chicken breast
Ingredients
    Good to know, this is the closest match found to the family's version, exact proportions and method aren't documented, so the dressing ratio and technique here are a reasonable starting point. Taste and adjust the yogurt-to-mayo balance to what tastes right, and if anyone remembers more details of how it was made, this is easy to update.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Make ahead, the chicken salad (without the lettuce and walnuts) can be made a day ahead and refrigerated, the flavor deepens overnight.

    Store, airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days.

    Assemble fresh, keep the lettuce, walnuts, and olive oil drizzle separate until serving so the lettuce stays crisp.
