Johanna's Meatloaf

Beef and pork, kept moist with a milk-soaked panade, topped with a tangy ketchup glaze that caramelizes in the oven. Comfort food, done right.

Receta de Johanna
ACTIVE 20 min BAKE 60 to 65 min SERVES 6 to 8
Servings
6
2 lbs beef, 0.5 lb pork
Ingredients
    Johanna's tip, don't overmix the meat, work it just until combined or the loaf turns dense. The milk-soaked breadcrumb panade is what keeps it moist, don't skip it.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Make ahead, the loaf can be shaped and refrigerated, unbaked, up to a day ahead. Glaze right before baking.

    Store, airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days.

    Reheat, sliced, covered, in a 300°F oven until warmed through, or microwave individual slices.

    Freeze, freezes well, cooked or uncooked, for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge before baking or reheating.
