Baked Sweet Potato

Puffed up, caramelized at the edges, crispy skin. The trick is leaving them bare on the sheet, not wrapped in foil, so the skin actually crisps instead of steaming soft.

ACTIVE 5 min ROAST 40 to 50 min SERVES 4
Servings
4
4 sweet potatoes
Ingredients
    Where to buy, Whole Foods is a good source for consistently sized, good quality sweet potatoes. Pick ones that are similar in size so they finish roasting around the same time.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Make ahead, bake a big batch and reheat through the week, they hold up well.

    Store, airtight container in the fridge for up to 5 days.

    Reheat, oven at 350°F until warmed through, or microwave for a quicker option.

    Leftover flesh, scoop out extra and use it in pancakes, hummus, or quesadillas, or blend it into a soup or sauce.
