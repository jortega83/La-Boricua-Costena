Creamy Gorgonzola Spaghetti

A one-pan Brazilian spaghetti, the noodles cook right in a caramelized onion and sun-dried tomato base with a splash of shoyu, finished with fresh cream, parmesan, and gorgonzola melted in at the end.

ACTIVE 20 min COOK 15 min SERVES 5
Servings
5
14 oz spaghetti
Ingredients
    The one-pan secret, everything cooks in the same pan, including the pasta itself, no separate pot to boil noodles. Keep a kettle or pot of boiling water on the side and add it in small splashes as the pasta drinks it up, never cold water, that stalls the cooking. Slicing the onions thin (half-moons) helps them melt down fully into the sauce, no strong onion bite left by the end.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Best fresh, this is a creamy, saucy pasta best eaten right away while it's loose and glossy.

    Store, airtight container in the fridge for up to 2 days.

    Reheat, stovetop over low heat with a splash of water or cream to loosen the sauce back up.

    Want protein? Crisped bacon or seared filet mignon, sliced thin, are great additions, stir in near the end.
