Curry Chicken Salad

Tender chicken, sweet grapes, and toasted cashews tossed in a creamy curry Greek yogurt dressing, no mayo. A fresh, bright twist on the classic, ready in 25 minutes.

PREP 15 min TOAST NUTS 10 min SERVES 6
Servings
6
2 cups diced chicken
Ingredients
    Good to know, don't walk away while the nuts toast, they can go from perfect to scorched in seconds. If the chicken or mix-ins seem watery, pat them dry first so the dressing doesn't get diluted. Almonds work in place of cashews.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Make ahead, this stores beautifully and is ready to go whenever you need lunch.

    Store, airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days.

    Freeze, can be frozen for up to 3 months, though the texture softens a bit once thawed. Thaw overnight in the fridge before serving.

    Serve it, in lettuce cups, on toasted bread, in a pita or tortilla, with crackers, or over a bed of greens.
