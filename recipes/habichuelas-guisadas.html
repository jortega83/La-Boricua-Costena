Habichuelas Guisadas

Pink beans stewed in a sofrito and tomato broth with ham, olives, and potatoes. The dish that belongs on the plate any time there's rice, quick enough for a weeknight.

ACTIVE 15 min SIMMER 15 min SERVES 6
Servings
6
1 can pink beans
Ingredients
    Good to know, pink beans (habichuelas rosadas) are traditional, but red kidney beans work well too. Calabaza (Caribbean pumpkin) can stand in for the potatoes if you want it extra creamy, it breaks down a bit as it cooks and thickens the broth.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Make ahead, tastes even better the next day once the flavors have had time to sit.

    Store, airtight container in the fridge for up to 5 days.

    Reheat, stovetop over low heat or microwave, with a splash of water if it's thickened up too much.

    Freeze, freezes wonderfully for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge before reheating.
