Buffalo Chicken Bowl

Diced chicken tossed in buffalo sauce over rice, with a cooling ranch yogurt drizzle, celery, and carrots. Spicy without being overwhelming.

ACTIVE 15 min COOK 12 min MAKES 4 bowls
Bowls
4
1.5 lbs chicken breast
Ingredients
    Good to know, toss the chicken in buffalo sauce right after cooking while it's still hot, it soaks in better. Blue cheese crumbles work in place of the ranch drizzle if you prefer.
    Rice ratio, this recipe always uses about 1¾ cups of water for every 1 cup of rice, plus ¼ teaspoon salt per cup of rice. That ratio is already built into the ingredient list above, whatever serving size you pick, the water and salt scale with it automatically.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Store, keep the chicken, rice, and vegetables in separate containers in the fridge for up to 4 days.

    Keep crisp, store the celery and carrots separately so they stay crunchy.

    Reheat, warm the chicken and rice in the microwave, then add the cold, fresh components and drizzle.
