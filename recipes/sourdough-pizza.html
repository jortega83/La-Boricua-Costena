Chris E's Sourdough Pizza

A tough, low-maintenance dough built in a stand mixer, cold-fermented overnight, then pan-fried and finished under the broiler for a charred base and bubbly top.

Receta de Chris E
FERMENT 3 to 5 hrs, then overnight PROOF 4 hrs MAKES 2 pizzas
Pizzas
2
320g flour, 50g starter
Ingredients
    Good to know, 00 pasta flour gives the best texture, but bread flour works too. No diastatic malt powder? A pinch of sugar or honey is a fine substitute. Once you're comfortable with this dough, a simpler way to scale it is by baker's percentage: starter 40%, water 60%, white 00 flour 80%, spelt 20%, salt 2.5%, all relative to total flour weight.
    Storage & keeping ⌄

    Make ahead, the overnight cold ferment is already built into this recipe, most of the work happens the day before.

    Store dough, shaped balls can stay in the fridge up to 2 days if you're not ready to bake, just bring to room temp and prove before using.

    Freeze, freeze shaped dough balls after the cold ferment, thaw overnight in the fridge, then continue with the final proof.
