Pescado con Coco

Firm white fish marinated in lime and cumin, simmered in a rich tomato-coconut sauce. Also known as Sudado de Pescado al Coco on the coast, best served over rice with yuca or patacones.

MARINATE 30 min COOK 22 min SERVES 4
Servings
4
2 lbs fish
Ingredients
    Good to know, swordfish, sea bass, trout, halibut, cod, red snapper, or even tilapia all work well here. For a variation, add shrimp, clams, or scallops to the stew along with or instead of the fish.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Make ahead, the sauce base can be made a day ahead and refrigerated, then reheated before adding fresh fish.

    Store, airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days.

    Reheat, gently on the stovetop over low heat so the fish doesn't overcook, or in the microwave covered.

    Freeze, the sauce freezes well on its own for up to 2 months, but freezing with the fish already in it can affect the texture. Best made fresh.
