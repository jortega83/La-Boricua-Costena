Lentejas Colombianas

A hearty lentil stew simmered with Colombian chorizo, carrots, and potato, popular in kitchens across the country including Caribbean coastal cities like Cartagena. Serve with white rice, patacones, and avocado.

ACTIVE 20 min SIMMER ~1 hr SERVES 5
Servings
5
1¼ cups dried lentils
Ingredients
    Good to know, Colombian chorizo is the real flavor driver here, look for it at a Latin market. If you can't find it, dried Spanish chorizo or a grilled Italian sausage both work well. The optional cilantro cream on top is a nice touch, but the soup is fully delicious without it too.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Make ahead, tastes even better the next day once the flavors settle.

    Store, airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days.

    Reheat, stovetop over low heat or microwave, thinning with a little water if it's thickened up.

    Freeze, freezes well for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge before reheating.
