Sopa de Pescado con Coco

A hearty coastal fish and shrimp chowder in a rich coconut broth, loaded with yuca, green plantain, and calabaza. A full meal in one pot, built from a whole fish for maximum flavor.

ACTIVE 40 min SIMMER 40 min SERVES 8
Servings
8
1 whole large fish, 2 lbs shrimp
Ingredients
    Good to know, sierra (king mackerel) is traditional here, but any firm, flavorful whole fish works. Cooking the fish whole, head included, gives the broth far more depth than using fillets alone. Culantro (not cilantro) has a stronger, more savory flavor, if you can't find it, use a bit more regular cilantro.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Make ahead, tastes even better the next day once the flavors settle.

    Store, airtight container in the fridge for up to 3 days.

    Reheat, gently on the stovetop over low heat so the shrimp doesn't overcook.

    Freeze, freezes well for up to 2 months, though the yuca and plantain soften further once thawed.
