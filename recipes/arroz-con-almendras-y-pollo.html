Arroz con Almendras

Fragrant rice cooked in rich chicken broth with caramelized onions, butter, cinnamon, and toasted almonds. A dish tied to Cartagena's Arab-Colombian culinary heritage, comforting and a little sweet from the cinnamon.

ACTIVE 30 min COOK 18 min SERVES 5
Servings
5
2 cups long-grain rice
Ingredients
    Good to know, toasting the rice in butter before adding the broth is what gives it that nutty depth, don't skip it. Caramelizing the onions low and slow with a pinch of cinnamon takes patience but makes the dish, don't rush that step. A cinnamon stick simmered in the broth adds even more warmth if you have one on hand.
    Meal prep & storage ⌄

    Make ahead, the broth, shredded chicken, and caramelized onions can all be made a day ahead and refrigerated separately.

    Store, airtight container in the fridge for up to 4 days.

    Reheat, stovetop or microwave with a splash of water or broth to loosen it back up.

    Freeze, freezes well for up to 2 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge before reheating.
